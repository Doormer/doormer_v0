Help with a question or your account

If something goes wrong, tell us what you were doing, what you saw, the page address, and a screenshot if you can. Please never send your password.

Report a wrong answer

If an answer looks wrong, tell us which question it was, which step looked wrong, and what you expected to happen. Reports help improve Doormer.

Privacy requests

You can ask to see, correct or delete personal information. Parents and guardians can ask on behalf of their child.

Schools and parents

Questions about how Doormer handles learning, ads and privacy are welcome. You may also want the AI homework help guide for parents or the parent section on the home page.
