Privacy policy

This policy explains what information Doormer collects, why, and the choices you have. We've written it to be read by students and parents.

Who we are

Doormer (“we”, “us”) runs the Doormer website and app. You can contact us at doormerapp@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account details. We collect your email address, password stored in a protected form, and your first name, last name and phone number if you give them when you register. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account ID from Google.

Question photos and solutions. We collect the question photos you upload and the solutions, steps, diagrams, topics and methods Doormer creates.

Learning activity. We collect questions you've solved and saved, answers you've opened, your quark balance and history, and your card collection.

Messages and technical information. We collect messages you send us. Our servers also collect technical information as they run, including IP address, browser and device type, pages requested, times and error logs.

How we use it

We use this information to run your account and sign you in, read and solve your questions, keep them in Saved, keep quarks and cards working, respond to messages, keep Doormer secure, fix problems, show the in-app ad that keeps Doormer free, and meet legal obligations.

We do not use personal information for selling.

How your photos are solved

Photos and question text are processed by Microsoft's Azure OpenAI Service to read and solve the question. They are stored in Microsoft Azure so you can see them again in Saved.

Under Microsoft's terms for this service, the data we send is not used to train OpenAI's models.

Ads and cookies

Website pages, including home, guides, about and legal pages, show no ads and set no cookies. In the app, while a photo is being solved, you may see one ad from Google AdSense.

Google's standard notice for sites that show its ads: third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to a website or to other websites, and Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to a website and/or other sites on the internet.

On Doormer, that kind of advertising is switched off. Doormer asks Google to treat every ad request as child-directed, which turns off ads based on browsing history (personalised ads) and remarketing. Google may still use cookies or similar technology for things like limiting how often an ad is shown, reporting and preventing fraud.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, and opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info. You can also read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Doormer's own cookies are two sign-in cookies that keep you logged in. One lasts up to 30 days. They are needed for the app to work. Doormer uses no analytics or advertising trackers of its own.

Who we share it with

We share information with service providers who run Doormer for us: Microsoft for Azure hosting, storage and the AI service, and Google for Google sign-in and ads in the app.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or as part of a sale or reorganisation of Doormer, with this policy still applying. We don't sell personal information.

Where it's stored and how we protect it

Information is stored with Microsoft Azure, which may be in data centres outside New Zealand and Australia. Connections use HTTPS. Azure encrypts stored data by default. Access is limited to people who need it.

No system is completely secure. Tell us if you think your account is at risk.

How long we keep it

We keep information while your account is open and as long as needed for the purposes above. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or de-identify your information unless the law requires us to keep it.

Students and parents

Doormer is designed for students aged 12 to 18. If you're under 16, ask a parent or guardian before creating an account. Parents and guardians can contact us to see or delete their child's information.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access or correct your personal information under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 or the Australian Privacy Act 1988. You can also ask us to delete your account. Email doormerapp@gmail.com.

If you're unhappy with our response, you can contact New Zealand's Office of the Privacy Commissioner or the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes to this policy

We'll update the date above when this policy changes. For significant changes, we'll tell you in the app or by email.

Contact us

Email doormerapp@gmail.com or use the contact page.
