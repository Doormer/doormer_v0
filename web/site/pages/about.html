About

Homework help that keeps you thinking

Doormer is a free web app for students in New Zealand and Australia. Take a photo of one homework question, and Doormer turns it into a step-by-step quest that helps you work towards the answer.

What Doormer does

Doormer reads the printed question in a photo and builds a quest from it. The quest starts with a plan, then opens one level at a time with a short step and a "Why this works" note.

When a picture helps, Doormer draws a diagram from the numbers and labels in the question. The final answer stays in a vault until the steps are done, then "Check it" shows a second way to test the answer.

Solved questions are kept in Saved, so students can come back before a test and practise the method again.

The problem we're solving

Answer-first tools can make copying easy and learning hard. That is not much help when the next question changes by one number, or when a teacher asks students to show their working.

Photos of messy worksheets can also confuse tools. Doormer is built to read the printed question, set aside student working, and ask for a clearer photo instead of guessing when the question cannot be read.

Students need structure and a reason to keep going. Doormer gives them a path through the question, not just the destination.

What we believe

  • Method before answer. The steps matter because they teach the move a student can use next time.
  • Be honest about limits. No AI is right every time, so Doormer checks its work and students should compare important answers with class notes or ask their teacher.
  • Rewards follow learning. Quarks and cards are just for fun after finishing questions.
  • Free for students. Doormer has no subscription and asks for no payment details.

Where we are

Doormer is in beta in New Zealand and Australia. We're improving it as we learn what students and parents need, and we'd love to hear from you.
